Port-to-door and door-to-port transport in Egypt

AK INTERNATIONAL coordinates land transport enquiries for commercial cargo moving between Egyptian ports, factories, warehouses and delivery addresses. Depending on the shipment, you can request port-to-door delivery, factory-to-port pickup, or an inland leg alongside international freight forwarding.

Give us the collection and delivery locations so we can review route, cargo handling and equipment requirements. Availability and timing depend on the actual cargo, location and operating arrangements; sending an enquiry does not confirm a truck booking.

Container trucking and commercial road freight

Container trucking requests may involve 20-foot or 40-foot units, including standard, high-cube or other equipment where suitable handling can be arranged. For non-containerised commercial cargo, supply the packaging type, number of packages, weight and dimensions so the transport requirement can be assessed.

Truck type, loading method, route constraints and any exceptional permits must be evaluated for each movement. Do not assume standard road equipment is suitable for oversized, hazardous or temperature-controlled cargo without advance confirmation.

Inland transport enquiries across Egyptian seaports

We welcome transport enquiries connected with Mediterranean and Red Sea gateways, including Alexandria, Dekheila, Damietta, East and West Port Said, Ain Sokhna, Suez, Adabiya, Safaga and Nuweiba. You may also enquire about other relevant Egyptian ports and logistics destinations. The available road connection and service scope are reviewed individually rather than guaranteed for every location.

For a combined movement, we can discuss the inland leg alongside sea freight forwarding in Egypt or customs clearance coordination. These are separate services, and the final route and commercial scope are confirmed during quotation.

Coordinating transport, warehousing and cargo release

Timing at the port depends on shipment readiness, customs procedures, terminal release and appointment or access requirements. Share the expected release status and any loading restrictions early so the proposed trucking schedule can be evaluated realistically.

Where the shipment needs an intermediate storage or delivery stage, ask about warehousing and distribution support or import and export documentation. Separate quotations or arrangements may apply.

What to include in your land transport quote request

  • Pickup location, delivery address and associated port, if applicable
  • Cargo description, package count, gross weight and dimensions
  • Container size and type, or the required cargo handling equipment
  • Preferred pickup date, shipment readiness and customs release status
  • Loading or unloading equipment, site access restrictions and special requirements

Use the AK INTERNATIONAL quotation form and choose land transport or add trucking to a freight enquiry. Our team can then review the transport options for the specified route.

Land transport: frequently asked questions

Can I ask about trucking through ports outside Alexandria or Port Said?

Yes. Submit your enquiry for any relevant Egyptian port, including Red Sea and Mediterranean locations. We will assess the requested route, equipment and operational availability before confirming the proposed service.

Can I request inland trucking without international sea freight?

Yes. Land transport can be requested separately, or alongside sea freight and customs services. Please provide the pickup and delivery points so the appropriate scope can be reviewed.
