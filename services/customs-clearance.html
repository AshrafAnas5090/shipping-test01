Customs Clearance Services in Egypt

AK INTERNATIONAL supports importers and exporters with customs documentation and shipment coordination in Egypt. Tell our operating team which port is involved, what cargo is moving and which documents are ready so we can review the next steps.

Import and export customs clearance

We work with businesses moving commercial cargo into and out of Egypt. For a useful clearance enquiry, identify the import or export direction, the intended Egyptian port, the commodity and the available shipping documents. The applicable process and approvals depend on the cargo, customs classification and shipment circumstances.

Customs documents and shipment readiness

Typical documents to review include the commercial invoice, packing list and transport document (bill of lading or air waybill). A certificate of origin, import or export authorisations, inspection records and other supporting papers may also be required depending on the goods. Include the HS code where available; any missing details can be identified during the enquiry.

Clear and consistent supplier, consignee, cargo and shipment details help reduce avoidable document queries, although no release date can be guaranteed in advance.

Nafeza, ACI and the ACID number

For imports covered by Egypt’s Advance Cargo Information (ACI) requirements, shipment information must be registered through Nafeza before shipping, and an Advance Cargo Information Declaration (ACID) reference is issued for the shipment. The importer or an authorised customs representative handles the relevant registration; the reference must be communicated to the parties preparing shipping documents.

Requirements and deadlines depend on shipment type and current regulations. Check the official Egypt Trade Portal guidance on ACID registration before dispatch. Our team can discuss the documents and coordination needed for your enquiry; submission or clearance is subject to applicable authorisations and regulatory checks.

Customs clearance enquiries across Egyptian ports

We welcome enquiries involving Egypt’s Mediterranean and Red Sea gateways, including Alexandria, Dekheila, Damietta, West Port Said, East Port Said, Ain Sokhna, Suez (Port Tawfik), Adabiya, Safaga and Nuweiba, as well as other Egyptian ports appropriate to the cargo.

The handling process, local requirements and available services are assessed for each shipment. Listing a port does not imply that AK INTERNATIONAL maintains a physical office there or that every cargo type is accepted at that port.

For integrated arrangements, explore sea freight, air freight and land transport.

What to include in your customs clearance enquiry

  • Import or export direction and the Egyptian clearance port
  • Product description, origin, approximate value and HS code if known
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and transport document status
  • ACI/ACID status for applicable imports and any permits or inspection needs
  • Expected arrival or departure date and any onward transport requirements

Frequently asked questions

Can I contact AK INTERNATIONAL before the documents are complete?

Yes. Share the documents you have and identify what is missing. We can review your request and clarify the information needed before confirming the possible service scope.

Are customs requirements identical at every Egyptian port?

No. Requirements depend on the goods, mode of transport, applicable regulations and the shipment. Port-specific handling must be confirmed for each case.
