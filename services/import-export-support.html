Import & Export Logistics Support in Egypt

Coordinate import and export shipment requirements with AK International, from cargo details and documentation to freight and delivery planning.

Discuss your shipment →

Coordinate the shipment requirements

Share the trade direction, route, goods description, readiness date and agreed delivery terms. This gives the team a starting point for discussing freight, documentation and additional services.

Keep responsibilities clear

Identify which parts of the shipment you need help with and which are already arranged. The service scope and responsibilities are confirmed for each request; website information does not replace transaction-specific review.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Import or export direction
  • Origin, destination and cargo description
  • Agreed delivery terms and readiness date
  • Freight, documentation, clearance or delivery support needed

Frequently asked question

Can several services be coordinated together?

Yes. Tell the team which services you need so the request can be reviewed as a connected shipment rather than separate enquiries.
